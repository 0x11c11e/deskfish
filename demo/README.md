# Demo

Three real recordings of Deskfish at work, unedited apart from trims at the start and end, silent, the chat sidebar on the left and its own desktop on the right. Each `.gif` is the same recording sped up for the README.

| File | Task | Length |
|---|---|---|
| `deskfish-writes-a-post.mp4` | 10 September 2026. Read what people on X are saying this week about agents that use a computer, draft a post from its own account, leave it unsent. | 2:13 |
| `deskfish-prices-a-trip.mp4` | 10 September 2026. The cheapest LAX to Hawaii fare for fixed dates; it compares every island on Google Flights, opens the winner, checks the return leg and flags the red-eye. The minute of typing the task is shown at 8×. | 2:03 |
| `deskfish-buys-its-domain.mp4` | 2 September 2026, its first task. Buy deskfish.sh for one year with no add-ons and knock on the glass at Pay; its human presses Pay, and it reads the confirmation back. | 4:09 |

The first two show nothing personal: the only account on screen is the project's own. In the domain recording everything personal is under a solid black box: the account name, the address and email, the card, the order number and payment reference, the checkout ids in the address bar, and the lines of chat that quoted them. Its first published cut (6 September) let some of that through and was withdrawn on 10 September; the current one was checked frame by frame, by several readers, before it went out.

`demo-poster.jpg` is the empty tank, the first frame of the domain recording.
