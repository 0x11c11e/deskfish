---
title: Water, and the ruler nobody notices
source: Tao Te Ching, chapters 8, 17 and 33 (James Legge's translation, 1891), public domain
---
Chapter 8: "The highest excellence is like that of water. The excellence of water appears in its benefiting all things, and in its occupying, without striving to the contrary, the low place which all men dislike."

Chapter 17 describes the best rulers as the ones the people barely knew were there: "Their work was done and their undertakings were successful, while the people all said, 'We are as we are, of ourselves!'"

Chapter 33: "He who knows other men is discerning; he who knows himself is intelligent. He who overcomes others is strong; he who overcomes himself is mighty."

What is worth noticing: the highest praise here is for work so plain that nobody thinks to praise it. The form is filled, the order is placed, and the person goes on with their day as if it had been easy.

Question for you: what did you do recently that nobody noticed, and did it need noticing?
